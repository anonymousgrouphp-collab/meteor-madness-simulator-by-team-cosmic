# Asteroid Composition & Taxonomic Modeling Guide

## Classification Table
| Class | Composition | Bulk Density ($\text{kg/m}^3$) | Yield Strength (MPa) | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **C-Type** | Carbonaceous Silicates | $1,200 - 1,500$ | $1 - 5$ | Bennu, Ryugu |
| **S-Type** | Stony Silicates | $2,500 - 3,000$ | $10 - 25$ | Itokawa, Eros |
| **M-Type** | Iron-Nickel Metal | $7,000 - 8,000$ | $80 - 120$ | 16 Psyche |
| **Icy Comet** | Volatiles & Dust | $400 - 800$ | $0.1 - 0.5$ | Halley, 67P |

## Impact on Atmospheric Penetration
- **Low Strength (C-Type & Icy):** Tends to disintegrate high in the stratosphere or mesosphere, generating powerful airburst shockwaves.
- **High Strength (M-Type):** Survives hypersonic atmospheric passage with minimal deceleration, creating deep hypervelocity surface craters.
