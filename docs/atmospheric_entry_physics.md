# Atmospheric Entry Physics

## 1. Drag Force Model
Meteoroids entering planetary atmospheres at hypersonic speeds ($v > 11 \text{ km/s}$)
experience substantial aerodynamic resistance governed by:

$$F_D = \frac{1}{2} \rho(h) v^2 C_D A$$

Where:
- $\rho(h)$: Local atmospheric density at altitude $h$
- $v$: Trajectory velocity relative to the atmosphere
- $C_D$: Drag coefficient (typically $1.2$ to $1.7$ for irregular spherical asteroids)
- $A$: Cross-sectional frontal area ($A = \pi r^2$)

## 2. Dynamic Stagnation Pressure
The stagnation pressure acting on the meteoroid leading face determines whether mechanical
fragmentation occurs before surface impact:

$$P_{stag} \approx \rho(h) v^2$$

When $P_{stag}$ exceeds the compressive mechanical yield strength of the asteroid material
($\sigma_{yield}$), catastrophic hydrodynamic disruption and airburst occurs.
