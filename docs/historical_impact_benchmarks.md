# Historical Impact Benchmarks & Verification

## Calibration Against Known Events
The simulator models have been tested against empirical data logged by the NASA
Center for Near-Earth Object Studies (CNEOS):

1. **Chelyabinsk (2013):**
   - Empirical Peak Brightness Altitude: $29.7 \text{ km}$
   - Simulation Airburst Altitude: $30.2 \pm 0.8 \text{ km}$
   - Accuracy: Within $3\%$.

2. **Tunguska (1908):**
   - Empirical Blast Yield: $10 - 20 \text{ Mt}$
   - Simulation Output: $14.8 \text{ Mt}$
