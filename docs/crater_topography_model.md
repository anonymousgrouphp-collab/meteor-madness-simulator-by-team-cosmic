# Impact Crater Morphology & Excavation

## Simple vs Complex Crater Regimes
On Earth, the transition diameter from simple bowl-shaped craters to complex central-peak
structures is approximately $3.2 \text{ km}$:
- **Simple Craters ($D < 3.2 \text{ km}$):** Depth $d \approx 0.2 D$.
- **Complex Craters ($D \ge 3.2 \text{ km}$):** Slumping of transient cavity walls creates flat-floored craters with central structural uplifts.
