# Supersonic Shockwave Physics

## Mach Cone Half-Angle
For an object moving at Mach number $M = v / c_s > 1$:

$$\sin(\mu) = \frac{1}{M}$$

Where $\mu$ is the Mach cone semi-vertex angle. As velocity increases, the Mach cone
narrows and concentrates aerodynamic energy into an intense conical discontinuity front.
