# Atmospheric Thermal Ablation Model

## 1. Classical Meteor Physics Equations
During entry, intense hypersonic shock compression converts kinetic energy into radiant thermal heat:

$$\frac{dm}{dt} = - \frac{\Lambda A \rho v^3}{2 Q}$$

Where:
- $\Lambda$: Dimensionless heat transfer coefficient ($0.05 - 0.20$)
- $Q$: Heat of ablation ($6 \times 10^6 \text{ J/kg}$ for stony meteoroids)
- $A$: Instantaneous frontal cross-section area
- $\rho$: Atmospheric mass density

## 2. Mass Fraction Retention
The fraction of original pre-atmospheric mass surviving until surface impact depends inversely
on the entry angle $\theta$ and velocity $v_0$.
