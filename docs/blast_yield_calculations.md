# Blast Yield & Impact Crater Scaling Laws

## 1. Kinetic Energy Conversion
The kinetic energy released during hypervelocity planetary entry:

$$E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$$

In TNT equivalent:
$$Y_{\text{TNT}} = \frac{E_k}{4.184 \times 10^{15} \text{ J/Mt}}$$

## 2. Shock Overpressure Radii
Blast overpressure thresholds for ground damage from airburst or surface detonation:
- **20 psi (138 kPa):** Complete structural destruction of reinforced concrete buildings.
- **5 psi (34.5 kPa):** Total collapse of residential wood and brick frames.
- **1 psi (6.9 kPa):** Glass window breakage and widespread minor lacerations.
